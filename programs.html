Our programs

Direct, hands-on service.

In a few months, we've run two programs reaching children and families across Sukkur.

Orphanage visit

A day of art and essentials

We visited a local orphanage and spent the day with the children. We brought art supplies and ran an art activity together, and supplied stationery and food items for the children to keep.

Ration drive

Ration packages for underprivileged families

We gathered contributions from our sponsors and used them to assemble ration packages for underprivileged families in Sukkur — each package built to last a family about a month.

More programs are on the way

As our team grows, so will the range of drives and campaigns we run. If you'd like to support what comes next, or join us directly, we'd love to hear from you.
